# The suggested email standard

This is the standard an enablement team would own. It is written here for
people; `scripts/lib/guidance.ts` enforces each line in code, and an email
that breaks any of them is not shown to the rep.

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| 50 to 125 words, 2 to 4 sentences | Long enough to say why, short enough to be read on a phone |
| At most one link | Links read as marketing and trip spam filters |
| Subject of at most 4 words, with the account name | Short subjects get opened; the name shows it was written for them |
| The account name in the body | Same reason |
| Exactly one question, at the end | One low effort next step gets an answer; two get neither |
| No claimed conversation unless one is on record | "As we discussed" with no call behind it is the fastest way to lose trust |
| No unfilled merge tokens | A "[First Name]" in a sent email is worse than no email |

When the plan has no safe contact with an address, or a high priority
support case is open, no email is suggested at all and the card says why.
